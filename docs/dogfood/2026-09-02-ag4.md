## Adversarial review

A Sol-max adversarial review found a pre-install race: helper or tunnel death
could arrive after `kernel_info` queued success but before the zero-delay
finalizer adopted the transport.  `34f1471` adds a synchronous transport
failure latch, fences already queued successes/events, correlates provisional
helper/tunnel ownership with the exact connect context, and revalidates both
resources at the installation boundary.  Deterministic tests cover helper
death, tunnel death, finalize-time revalidation, and the reentrant
success-then-failure ordering.

The same review drove the gate hardening recorded above: exact code/status
matching, repeated source immutability assertions, early cleanup identity
capture, exact session-file cleanup, and PID/cmdline ownership checks that
prevent the isolated Doom runner from terminating an unowned daemon.

`98faa58` then hardened the destructive replacement boundary.  Retry-fresh is
an exclusive transaction: it retains the exact registry entry until a bounded,
PID-and-argv-bound remote cleanup proves both process death and removal of the
JSON/log/PID paths.  Cancellation, timeout, malformed output, permission
denial, registry retirement failure, and stale callbacks start no replacement
and retain recovery authority.  Registry pruning now groups by the complete
generated SSH route and accepts a dead result only from a strict tri-state
response with one record per requested positive PID and one final terminator.
Missing, duplicate, contradictory, out-of-set, or otherwise malformed records
make the entire route unknown.

## Manual gaps

AG4 is not complete yet.  The following require real graphical use over time:

- Hours-long Wi-Fi loss and restoration while the registered kernel remains
  alive, including the idle-watchdog interaction.
- Real laptop sleep/resume.
- A multi-minute busy cell across a transport outage.
- Repeated reconnect cycles beyond the isolated helper/tunnel fault pair.
- Rapid scrolling over 100+ images in a graphical Doom frame, including native
  image-cache/RSS observation.  AG3 covers bounded retained state headlessly,
  not graphical cache behavior.
- Migration of the user's real Doom profile from `:jupyter-command` to the
  structured `:python-command` argv used above.
- At least one week of normal helper-default use before AG5 may remove the
  legacy emacs-jupyter transport.
